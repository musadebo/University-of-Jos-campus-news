## Copyright and Usage Guidelines

### Preferred Sources:
1. **Official UNIJOS website** (`unijos.edu.ng`) with proper attribution
2. **Creative Commons licensed** UNIJOS images
3. **Wikipedia Commons** University of Jos images (if available)
4. **Official UNIJOS social media** with permission

### Usage Requirements:
- Must clearly represent actual University of Jos facilities
- Should maintain the architectural and environmental character of UNIJOS
- Must comply with copyright and fair use guidelines
- Attribution required where specified by source

### Fallback Strategy:
- Use architectural placeholder images with clear comments
- Indicate "PLACEHOLDER - Needs actual UNIJOS image"
- Maintain proper image dimensions and quality standards
- Ensure images work well with glassmorphism overlay effects
